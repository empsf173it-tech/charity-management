Help Center

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about donating, volunteering, and making a lasting difference with KindHeart.

Donations

Giving & Payments

You can donate through our Contact / Donate page. We accept bank transfers, credit/debit cards, and digital wallets. Simply fill in the form, select your amount, and our team will reach out to confirm the payment.

Yes. KindHeart is a registered non-profit organisation. Donations are eligible for tax deductions in accordance with local regulations. You will receive an official receipt within 48 hours of your contribution that can be used for tax filing purposes.

Absolutely! After your first donation, you can log in to your donor account and enable a recurring monthly plan. Choose the amount and the charge date, and we will handle the rest. You can pause or cancel at any time.

There is no strict minimum. Even $5 can provide a day's worth of meals for a child. Our interactive donation calculator on the Home page shows exactly how your amount helps.

Yes. During the donation process, select "In Memory / In Honor" and provide the name. We will send a tribute acknowledgement card to a recipient of your choice and publish a mention on our Honor Wall (with your permission).
Volunteering

Joining Our Team

Visit our Volunteer Registration page, complete the form with your skills and availability, and our coordinator will match you with a suitable program within 3–5 business days.

No prior experience is required for most of our programs. We provide orientation training and ongoing mentorship. Whether you are a student, professional, or retiree, there is a meaningful role for you.

Yes! We offer fully remote volunteer opportunities including online tutoring, content creation, social media support, fundraising coordination, and data entry. See our Home page for upcoming remote events.

Yes. After completing 10 or more hours of service, you will automatically receive a digitally signed certificate of appreciation from KindHeart. Certificates are downloadable from your dashboard.
Programs

Our Initiatives

We run 8 core programs: Child Education Sponsorship, Clean Water Initiative, Emergency Food Relief, Women's Skills Development, Healthcare Outreach, Environmental Reforestation, Tech for Youth, and Elderly Care Support. Visit our Programs page to explore each in detail.

Programs are selected based on community needs assessments conducted annually. Funding is pooled from donations and grants, then allocated according to impact metrics and geographic priority. All funding decisions are published in our annual transparency report.

We currently operate active programs in 18 countries across South Asia, Sub-Saharan Africa, and Latin America. Our volunteer impact map (available on our Home page) shows live program locations.
Account & Login

Your KindHeart Account

Click Sign Up at the top of any page. You can register with your email or use Google/Facebook OAuth. Account setup takes under 2 minutes.

On the Login page, click "Forgot Password". Enter your registered email, and you will receive a reset link within a few minutes. Check your spam folder if you don't see it.

Log in and visit your Dashboard. The "Donations" section shows a full history with dates, amounts, and programs supported. You can also download PDF receipts for each transaction.
Transparency

Trust & Accountability

At least 85% of every donation goes directly to program delivery — food, books, water infrastructure, and medical aid. The remaining 15% covers verified administrative and logistics costs. Our audited annual report is publicly available on request.

Yes. KindHeart undergoes an independent third-party financial audit every year. Results are shared with registered donors and published in our annual transparency report. We are also registered with GuideStar/Candid and maintain a Platinum Seal of Transparency.

We take all concerns seriously. Use the confidential reporting form on our Contact page or email integrity@kindheart.org. All reports are reviewed within 5 business days and handled with full confidentiality.

Still have questions?

Our support team is here Monday – Friday, 9 AM – 6 PM. We usually respond within a few hours.
